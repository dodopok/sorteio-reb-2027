# Imagens do Kit Anglicano

As imagens da caneca e do item surpresa foram geradas com a ferramenta integrada ImageGen. A capa de O Caminho Anglicano e a arte enviada pelo organizador foram preservadas em public/images/kit/. A composição final é feita em app/components/KitMockup.vue, com proporções originais das imagens e fundo transparente.

Arquivos usados na página: anglican-book.webp (conversão sem perda da capa), anglican-mug.webp e mystery-book.webp (codificação WebP, qualidade 90, transparência preservada). Os PNGs originais também ficam na pasta para referência. As imagens geradas são mockups de divulgação; o livro embrulhado apenas representa o item surpresa.

## Prompt da caneca

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent product photograph for the Kit Anglicano raffle website. Generate ONE photorealistic, ordinary white ceramic coffee mug, standard 325 ml / 11 oz proportions, standing straight upright, normal round cylindrical body, a realistic C-shaped handle on the right, softly rounded rim. Input image 1 is the exact blue print artwork to place on the front: use only the inner white square and blue artwork, discard the patterned cream screenshot border. Preserve the two cartoon Anglican clergy and staff and preserve the words EXACTLY: "SEJA ANGLICANO" above, "GOSTOSO DEMAIS" below. Do not invent or redraw any extra graphic. Print should be crisp and naturally conform very subtly to the curvature, entirely readable, without stretched or squashed letters. Real studio product photography with soft light, natural white ceramic gloss, gentle realistic reflections. Camera almost straight-on, slightly above rim, 85mm product lens, mild perspective; mug should look normal and credible, not stylized, not illustrated, not a vector rendering, not wide or squat. Transparent alpha background, fully isolated, no table, no backdrop, no extra objects, no cast shadow outside the mug. Include the complete mug and handle with a tight, even transparent margin. A single mug only, no collage, no books. Production-ready e-commerce cutout.

## Prompt do item surpresa

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent product photograph for a raffle kit. Generate ONE realistic mysterious book wrapped in warm ivory kraft paper, upright, an ordinary book about 21cm tall, 14cm wide, 2cm thick. The book itself must stay hidden. High quality but simple tactile gift wrapping: fine natural paper fibers, convincing folds along the edges, a narrow burgundy-plum satin ribbon crossing vertically and horizontally, a small ivory paper tag at center with ONLY a dark burgundy "?" question mark. No other words or logos. The wrapped book should look like a real photographed object, not a vector illustration or a flat geometric drawing. Studio product photography, softly lit from upper left, subtle material shading and believable rounded folded edges. Nearly frontal camera with a mild three-quarter angle showing a small left spine edge and top edge, compressed perspective of an 85mm product lens. Full object visible with tight even transparent margins. Transparent alpha background, isolated cutout, no table, no backdrop, no cast shadow beyond object, no extra objects, no mug, no collage, no floating ornaments or glow. The composition is for a prize kit with a white ceramic mug and a printed book cover: premium, natural and understated.

## Edição do recorte do item surpresa

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied photograph of the ivory-paper wrapped mystery book. REMOVE ALL of the blurred brown/gray backdrop and glow surrounding the object. Keep ONLY the wrapped book, ribbon, bow and question-mark tag exactly as shown; preserve the photorealistic materials, geometry, colors and lighting of the object. The book itself must be fully opaque. Everything outside the precise silhouette of the wrapped book must be fully transparent alpha, including all blurred halos and shadows. A clean e-commerce cutout with tight even margins. Do not regenerate the book or add objects. No matte rectangle, no gray fringe, no background gradient, no shadow outside the book.
